# High Availability and Redundancy for Video Management Software (VMS) {#high-availability-and-redundancy-for-video-management-software-vms}

##  What is video management software (VMS)? {#what-is-video-management-software-vms}

Video management software (VMS) collects and records camera video. It provides an interface for operators to view live and recorded video.

Video management software increases life safety and reduces theft. It also improves the efficiency of security officers.

##  Why is VMS software critical? {#why-a-vms-software-is-critical}

VMS software records all criminal acts, from theft to murder. It also helps to track criminals.

The safety of people depends directly on the VMS software.

Security officers must react immediately to the slightest incident. The VMS software must therefore always be available to them.
