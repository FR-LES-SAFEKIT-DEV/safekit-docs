{{/* Shortcode: insert-safekit-use-cases-en — 2 instances in 2 files */}} {{/* Parameters: none */}} {{/* Source: content\use-cases\_index.md */}} ## SafeKit Use Cases {#safekit-use-cases} ### SafeKit for OEM {#safekit-for-oem} Offering high availability with your application increases business value. It ensures continuous service, reduces downtime risks, and enhances customer trust. Critical operations run without interruption on standard infrastructures. ![SafeKit for OEM](/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/safekit-for-oem-512-512.jpg) You can add SafeKit to your catalog as a high availability option. It is a software-only solution tailored to your application. There are no hidden costs such as shared storage. It is fully hardware-agnostic and runs on physical, virtual, or cloud environments. Administration is simple and plug-and-play. 📚 **Related pages**: [High Availability for OEMs: SafeKit Clustering Embedded in Your Software](/use-cases/application-clustering-software/) ### SafeKit for Edge {#safekit-for-edge} Edge sites often have no data center and no HA expertise. Yet business continuity is critical. SafeKit keeps edge applications running. Examples are factories, oil platforms, ships, building security, air traffic control, 5G networks, healthcare, and retail. ![SafeKit for Edge](/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/safekit-edge-512-512.jpg) SafeKit turns two standard edge servers (any brand) into a plug-and-play HA cluster. It needs no shared storage and no SAN. One lightweight stack delivers real-time replication and automatic failover. It can also include load balancing. It is easy to install and administer. 📚 **Related pages**: [Edge High Availability Without a Data Center](/use-cases/edge-high-availability-without-a-data-center-safekit-for-ot-distributed-sites/) ### SafeKit for VMS {#safekit-for-vms} Video Management Software (VMS) is critical to public safety. It records and displays live and archived video, so security officers can react instantly to incidents. Any VMS outage directly puts people and assets at risk. ![SafeKit for VMS](/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/safekit-vms-512-512.jpg) SafeKit prevents video loss and monitoring gaps by maintaining continuous access to live and recorded streams, even during server or software failures. It integrates seamlessly with leading VMS platforms and keeps surveillance operational when it matters most. Examples are **Milestone, Genetec, and Hanwha**. 📚 **Related pages**: [VMS Redundancy for Video Management Software](/use-cases/video-management-software-vms-redundancy/) ### SafeKit for EACS {#safekit-for-eacs} Electronic Access Control Systems (EACS) are essential to physical security. They control and monitor access to private and sensitive areas through doors, badges, readers, and sensors. Any system outage can immediately expose people, buildings, and assets to intrusion. ![SafeKit for EACS](/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/safekit-eacs-512-512.jpg) SafeKit keeps access control decisions, alarms, and credentials available at all times by eliminating single points of failure. It delivers resilient operation for EACS solutions. Examples are **Hirsch Microsesame, Nedap AEOS, and Siemens SiPass**. Access stays secure even during infrastructure incidents. 📚 **Related pages**: [EACS Redundancy for Electronic Access Control](/use-cases/electronic-access-control-software-eacs-redundancy/) ### SafeKit for SCADA {#safekit-for-scada} SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition) systems are at the core of industrial environments. Operators use them to monitor and control critical processes through sensors, valves, pumps, motors, and human-machine interfaces. ![SafeKit for SCADA](/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/safekit-scada-512-512.jpg) SafeKit minimizes production downtime. SCADA control systems stay operational despite hardware or software incidents. Examples are the systems that run **Probat coffee roasters and ALSTEF baggage sorting machines**. Operators keep full visibility and control of industrial processes at all times. This prevents costly shutdowns and safety risks. 📚 **Related pages**: [SCADA Software Redundancy and High Availability](/use-cases/scada-software-redundancy-high-availability/) ### SafeKit for BMS {#safekit-for-bms} Building Management Systems (BMS) are central to modern buildings. They provide automated control of HVAC, electrical distribution, lighting, fire safety, and water systems. Any system outage can directly impact occupant safety, comfort, and building operations. ![SafeKit for BMS](/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/safekit-bms-512-512.jpg) SafeKit safeguards building automation. BMS services continue running transparently in the event of a failure. It supports platforms such as **Siemens Desigo CC and Bosch BIS**, and related systems. Building operations stay safe, efficient, and uninterrupted. 📚 **Related pages**: [BMS Redundancy for Building Management Systems](/use-cases/building-management-system-software-bms-redundancy/) ### SafeKit for ATC {#safekit-for-atc} Air Traffic Control (ATC) systems are critical to aviation safety. They enable real-time monitoring and control of aircraft movements on the ground and in the air. They use surveillance, guidance, and control applications. ![SafeKit for ATC](/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/safekit-atc-512-512.jpg) SafeKit reinforces ATC system resilience. Controllers keep uninterrupted access to critical airside applications. SafeKit works with ATC and airport solutions. It supports safe, continuous air traffic operations under all conditions. An example is **ADB SafeGate**. 📚 **Related pages**: [High Availability for Air Traffic Control Systems](/use-cases/high-availability-air-traffic-control-system/) ### SafeKit for OCC {#safekit-for-occ} Operations Control Centers (OCC) are at the heart of modern metro networks. They centralize the supervision of train movements, power supply, signaling, passenger information, and incident management. In automated driverless metro lines, the OCC is the single point of control for operations. ![SafeKit for OCC](/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/safekit-occ-512-512.jpg) SafeKit secures uninterrupted supervision of metro lines. OCC applications remain available during failures. SafeKit supports the Operations Control Centers of the **automated, driverless Paris metro lines**. This enables continuous service and rapid incident response without onboard drivers. 📚 **Related pages**: [High Availability for Public Transport Control Rooms](/use-cases/high-availability-public-transport-centralized-control-room/)
